How a trade actually gets picked
Every M1 candle close, all six candidates above (S1/S2/S3 × BUY/SELL) are evaluated. Any that clear every gate for their own strategy become eligible; the eligible candidate with the single highest 0–100 quality score is the one that fires — so a high-quality Strategy 2 or 3 setup can outrank a weaker Strategy 1 breakout, and vice versa. Only one trade is opened per signal. The chart's top-left gate-pill overlay always shows whichever of the six candidates has progressed furthest, labeled with the strategy it belongs to.
